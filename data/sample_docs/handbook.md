# Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Northwind Analytics operates on a flexible schedule. Core collaboration hours are
10:00 to 15:00 local time, and employees are expected to be reachable during that
window. Outside core hours, engineers may arrange their time as they see fit as long
as they deliver on their sprint commitments.

## Remote work policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require manager approval and a signed equipment agreement. The company provides a
one-time home-office stipend of 500 USD and a monthly internet allowance of 40 USD.

## Vacation and leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 public
holidays. Unused vacation of up to 5 days may be carried into the first quarter of
the following year. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for all parents.

## Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD that can be spent on
courses, books, conferences, and certifications. Popular internal study groups
cover machine learning, distributed systems, and technical writing.

## Expense reimbursement
Expenses below 100 USD are approved by a direct manager. Anything above 100 USD
requires director approval. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days through the
finance portal.
